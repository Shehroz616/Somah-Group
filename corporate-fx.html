Structured Hedging - Protection with upside

Forwards
Protection only — you lock a rate and must transact at that level regardless of where the market moves.

Structured Hedging
Protection and participation — you are protected if the market moves against you, while still benefiting if it moves in your favour.

Structured hedging protects downside while allowing participation if the market moves in your favour within a defined range.

  • • Downside protection
  • • Upside participation
  • • Tailored to exposure
  • • Built for recurring or larger volumes
