ABOUT SOMAH GROUP

Institutional Excellence in Global Capital Markets

Founded on principles of institutional rigor and client partnership, Somah Group delivers sophisticated capital markets solutions across foreign exchange, credit, and cross-border liquidity for institutional clients worldwide.

We do not operate as a single-provider firm. Our model is built around understanding each client’s requirement and matching it to the most appropriate solution — whether through existing partner access or by sourcing new pathways through our broader network.

Aligned Incentives

Our clients do not pay us directly.

We are compensated by the providers within our network — meaning our success is directly linked to long-term client satisfaction.

  • If a solution is not suitable, it will not be used.
  • If it is not used, we do not earn.

This ensures our focus remains on placing clients with the right partners — not simply completing transactions.

"The objective is not to complete a transaction — it is to place clients with the right long-term solution."
