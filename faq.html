Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Answers to the most common questions about onboarding, service coverage, FX facilities and client protections.

How fast can I get set up?

Most clients can be onboarded within 24–48 hours, subject to completion of required documentation for FX services.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. We work with businesses ranging from growing SMEs to larger enterprises, typically from £1m up to £100m annual turnover.

What is Pre-Funded FX?

Pre-Funded FX is a facility that allows your overseas supplier to be paid upfront, while you settle the FX payment at a later agreed date. Payment terms can be up to 150 days.

How are funds safeguarded?

Client funds are held with FCA-regulated payment partners and safeguarded with Tier-one banking institutions in line with UK regulatory requirements.

Can I use Somah Group for both FX and trade finance?

Yes. Somah Group connects clients to partners across FX payments, risk management, invoice finance and trade finance solutions.
