Structured Hedging

Advanced derivative strategies tailored to protect margins and optimize risk in volatile market conditions.

"The objective is not to complete a transaction — it is to place clients with the right long-term solution."

Problem

Suboptimal Hedging Habits.

Most businesses default to one of three poor approaches to managing currency exposure, resulting in unnecessary, unrewarded risk.

This leads to missed opportunities and immense margin pressure.

They Don’t Hedge

Leaving the entire bottom line exposed to unpredictable market volatility.

They Over-Hedge

Locking up excessive liquidity, tying their hands, and limiting potential upside completely.

Or use basic forwards only

Using blunt instruments exclusively for complex, nuanced pricing risks.

Solution

Access to structured hedging strategies via institutional partners.

Used correctly, these sophisticated instruments fundamentally change the mechanics of your returns.

How Structured Hedging Differs

Traditional hedging (forwards) provides certainty — but removes upside.

Structured hedging allows:

  • Protection when the market moves against you

  • Participation when the market moves in your favour (within defined parameters)

Simple Example

A business buying USD:

  • With a forward → you lock a fixed rate

  • With structured hedging → you secure protection, while retaining the ability to benefit if the market improves within a range

Outcome

  • Improved average exchange rate over time
  • Better margin retention
  • More flexible treasury strategy

"When structured correctly and priced through the right counterparties, these solutions can materially improve outcomes versus standard hedging alone."
