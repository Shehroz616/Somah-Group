Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 16, 2026

Introduction

Somah Group respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, submit inquiries, or engage with our institutional capital markets services.

We act as a capital markets intermediary providing access to foreign exchange, commercial finance, and cross-border liquidity solutions. This policy applies to all users of our website and services.

Information We Collect

Personal Data You Provide

  • Full name, work email, company name, and contact details when you submit an inquiry form.
  • Business-related information such as your role, organization type, and institutional requirements.
  • Any additional details you voluntarily provide during communications or consultations.

Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address, browser type, device information, and usage data via cookies and analytics tools.
  • Pages visited, time spent on site, and referral sources.

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Responding to your inquiries and providing information about our capabilities and services.
  • Facilitating introductions to liquidity providers, lenders, or execution partners.
  • Improving our website, services, and user experience.
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations in the financial services sector.
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring the security of our platform.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only in these limited circumstances:

  • With carefully selected institutional providers or counterparties when you request a connection (with your consent or as necessary to fulfill the service).
  • With service providers who assist us (e.g., hosting, analytics, email services) under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • When required by law, regulation, or court order (including financial regulators).
  • In the event of a business transfer or merger (with appropriate protections).

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including encryption, access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws (including GDPR if relevant), you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request data portability.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses essential cookies for functionality and analytics cookies to understand traffic and improve user experience. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

International Transfers

As an international business, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your jurisdiction. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised accordingly. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
