Traditional hedging (forwards) provides certainty ΓÇö but removes upside.
Structured hedging allows:
ΓÇó 	Protection when the market moves against you
ΓÇó 	Participation when the market moves in your favour (within defined parameters)
Γ╕╗

A business buying USD:
ΓÇó 	With a forward ΓåÆ you lock a fixed rate
ΓÇó 	With structured hedging ΓåÆ you secure protection, while retaining the ability to

Γ╕╗

ΓÇó 	Improved average exchange rate over time
ΓÇó 	Better margin retention
ΓÇó 	More flexible treasury strategy
Γ╕╗
